The Hidden Cost of Ghosting in Recruitment

Ghosting isn't just a candidate behaviour — it's a symptom. When candidates ghost employers, it signals a disconnect in how organisations communicate, engage, and value talent at the earliest stage of the relationship.

The cost goes far beyond a wasted interview slot. There's the recruiter's time, the hiring manager's preparation, the delay in onboarding, and perhaps most critically — the signal it sends about your employer brand in a connected talent market.
