Our Services Range & Products

Service 01

Talent Fulfilment

From frontline hiring to leadership search — we cover every layer of your talent pyramid. Our specialist teams operate across verticals with deep sourcing intelligence, behavioural assessment frameworks, and a network built over decades.

We understand that every open position has a cost — a financial cost, an opportunity cost, and a cultural cost. Our mandate is to reduce time-to-fill without compromising on quality of hire. Whether it's bulk hiring for a new facility or a single C-suite search, we bring the same rigour to every brief.

Organizations are coping with hard-to-fill roles, which go up to 120+ days of ageing. Most times, organisations accept a partial compromise. We have a dedicated specialist team for such situations.

— Swati Sonkar, Managing Director
What's Included
Frontline Hiring

High-volume entry-level recruitment with quality screening, reducing cost-per-hire while maintaining fit.

Bulk Acquisition

Large-scale hiring drives for new operations, expansions, or project-based manpower requirements.

Stretched Acquisition

When the talent pool is thin and conventional sourcing fails — we stretch beyond conventional channels.

Niche Hiring

Highly specialised roles that age beyond 90–120 days across other consultants. Our dedicated niche team solves the unsolvable.

Mid-Level Hiring

Manager-to-Senior Manager roles requiring precise functional depth and cultural alignment assessment.

Leadership Search

CXO, Director and VP-level search assignments — discreet, thorough, and aligned with long-term business strategy.

Service 02

Learning & Development

Great organisations invest in their people's growth. Our L&D portfolio spans the full development spectrum — from building foundational soft skills to transforming how leaders think and communicate. Every intervention is customised: we design for your specific context, your culture, and your desired outcomes.

We don't deliver training. We deliver change in capability. Our faculty brings together practitioners from industry, not theoreticians. The result is learning that is immediately applicable and produces visible behavioural shifts.

The best L&D happens when learners don't feel they are being trained — they feel they are discovering something true about themselves and their work.

— Amazing HR Learning Consortium
What's Included
Behavioural & Soft Skills Training

Communication, teamwork, conflict resolution, time management, and interpersonal effectiveness programmes.

Voice & Accent Training

Specialised programmes for customer-facing teams — improving articulation, pronunciation, and spoken confidence.

TQM Trainings

Total Quality Management frameworks adapted for HR and people-function teams to embed a quality mindset.

Leadership Development & Executive Coaching

1:1 and cohort-based programmes for senior leaders — building self-awareness, strategic thinking, and executive presence.

Customised Training Interventions

Bespoke programmes designed from scratch to address your organisation's unique capability gaps and development priorities.

Service 03

OD Interventions

Organisation Development is about creating the conditions in which people and businesses thrive together. Our OD practice works at the intersection of people, process, and culture — diagnosing gaps, designing interventions, and embedding sustainable change.

Whether you need to understand how engaged your workforce truly is, map competencies for a new business direction, or audit the entire HR function — our OD specialists bring both the methodology and the experience to deliver real, lasting impact.

Organisation development is not about fixing people — it's about building the architecture in which people can do their best work consistently.

— Amazing HR OD Practice
What's Included
Competency Mapping & Assessment/Development Centres

Defining, assessing, and developing competency frameworks aligned to business strategy — for hiring, development, and succession.

Engagement Surveys

Quantitative and qualitative surveys that reveal the true pulse of your organisation — with actionable analysis for leadership.

Benchmarking Exercises

Comparing your HR metrics against industry peers — compensation, attrition, engagement — to identify gaps and opportunities.

Best Practices Interventions

Identifying and embedding globally recognised HR best practices adapted and contextualised for your organisation.

HR Process Automation

Mapping, redesigning, and automating HR workflows to eliminate inefficiency and reduce manual dependency.

HR Audit

Comprehensive audit of your HR function — processes, compliance, systems, capability, and alignment to business objectives.

Service 04

HR Systems & Processes

The backbone of a high-performance organisation is its HR infrastructure. Great policies, transparent processes, robust compliance, and well-designed systems make the difference between HR as an administrative cost centre and HR as a strategic business driver.

We design, redesign, and optimise the full stack of HR systems and processes — from the HR manual to performance philosophy to compensation architecture. We also support businesses through complex compliance and investigative mandates.

Well-designed HR systems reduce noise, resolve ambiguity, and let managers focus on leading people rather than managing paperwork and process confusion.

— Amazing HR Systems Practice
What's Included
HR Manual & Policies Design

Comprehensive, legally sound, and culture-aligned HR manuals and policy frameworks from scratch or as a redesign exercise.

Performance Management

Designing PM systems that are transparent, fair, motivating, and aligned to strategic goals — not just annual review rituals.

Compensation Management

Total rewards architecture, salary benchmarking, grade structures, and incentive design to attract and retain top talent.

HR Compliances & Legal Support

Ensuring full statutory compliance across labour laws — PF, ESIC, Shops & Establishment, Factories Act, and more.

Domestic Enquiry, POSH & Executive Investigations

Conducting fair, legally compliant domestic enquiries, POSH complaints, and sensitive executive-level investigations.

Retention Management

Diagnosing attrition drivers and designing targeted retention strategies for critical talent cohorts.

Job Analysis & Evaluation

Rigorous job analysis, role definition, and evaluation to build an equitable and well-structured job architecture.

Service 05

Innovation & Customised Solutions

This is where Amazing HR is truly ahead of the times. We have developed a portfolio of proprietary and bespoke solutions that address the gaps conventional HR consulting leaves behind. Solutions built from first principles, not adapted from textbooks.

From our groundbreaking Ghosting Processes that give leadership anonymous, real-time insight into candidate and employee sentiment, to HR analytics that convert people data into strategic decisions — this is the future of HR, available today.

Every organisation has inherent needs to address which are seldom stated, though felt by everyone. Our automation solutions map the desired process flow and integrate it — making the system healthy and defect-free, immediately.

— Vani Tyagi, Director

Ghosting Processes

Power of Psychometric Assessments

Employer Brand Meter

Measuring and strengthening your employer brand — understanding how you are perceived by current employees, candidates, and the talent market.

Employee Delight

Moving beyond employee satisfaction to design experiences that create advocates — people who stay and inspire others to join.

Human Capital Accounting

Quantifying the value of your people assets — enabling CFOs and CHROs to make investment decisions in talent backed by data.

Research & HR Analytics

Custom HR research, workforce analytics, attrition prediction, and data-driven insights that transform how leadership understands its people.
